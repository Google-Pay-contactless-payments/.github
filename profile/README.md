# Google Pay - Payment Service

## About Google Pay

Google Pay is a payment service built for everyday money movement on phones, tablets, and browsers. It brings together Google Pay contactless payments, Google Pay send money transfers, and Google Pay online checkout so people can pay, receive, and track activity without jumping between separate tools. Google Pay keeps cards, transactions, and confirmation screens in one organized place, which makes daily spending easier to follow for commuters, online shoppers, families, and anyone who prefers a simple payment routine.
